# Upwing Energy

> Upwing Energy is an energy technology company (founded 2016, Cerritos, California,
> with service operations near Houston, Texas) and an affiliate of Calnetix Technologies.
> It builds the Subsurface Compressor System (SCS) — a downhole gas compressor deployed
> up to two miles inside a natural gas well — plus Magnetic Drive Systems and the
> high-speed turbomachinery that powers them. It sells hardware and field services to
> natural gas operators, not software.

## API surface

**Upwing Energy publishes no API.** As of 2026-09-02 there is no developer program,
no public API, no SDK, no webhook catalog and no machine-readable specification of
any kind (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf, WSDL, JSON Schema, MCP server or
A2A agent card). This is a completed measurement, not a gap in the search:

- The site's own sitemap.xml (HTTP 200) enumerates ~90 pages — products, innovations,
  impact, careers, press, legal — and contains no developer, docs, API or portal page.
- /openapi.json, /swagger.json, /api-docs, /docs, /graphql, /llms.txt and all seven
  probed /.well-known/ paths return HTTP 404 with the site's HTML 404 page.
- api.upwingenergy.com, developer.upwingenergy.com and docs.upwingenergy.com do not
  resolve in DNS.
- The GitHub organization https://github.com/Upwing-Energy exists but has zero public
  repositories.
- No first-party client library exists on npm, PyPI or any other public registry.

Customer telemetry from a deployed SCS is handled as a managed service, not as a
product API: the operations-and-monitoring page states data is "sent and stored in the
cloud for engineers to download and review in real-time" and that "Upwing can also push
the available data in a format and frequency preferred by the client". That is a
bilateral data-delivery arrangement, not a published contract.

- Subsurface Compressor System (SCS) — downhole gas compression, deployed in-well

- Hermetically sealed high-speed permanent magnet motors
- Passive magnetic radial bearings; active magnetic thrust bearings
- Magnetic couplings
- Sensorless long-step-out variable speed drives
